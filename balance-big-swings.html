To build it
  • Page 1 switches on user.onboarding_goal_1. Users who picked focus (10% of goal answers) or gave no answer see the generic version (Balance's current screen: "Start a free week that's built around how you feel").
  • The 30-day option needs a new annual product with a one-month pay-up-front introductory offer. An App Store product carries one intro offer at a time, and the current annual product already uses its offer for the 7-day trial. $4.99 is a placeholder price.
  • No "View all plans" link: the page offers only the two trial options. Closing still shows the control's exit offer.
B

More visual

onboarding_goal_1 on page 3

The same three-page story, told with motion. Page 1 animates the shift from a grey, rainy morning to a calm, sunny one. Page 2 is the control's reminder choice with a notification that drops in and rings. Page 3 keeps the control's offer (yearly with the 7-day trial upfront, monthly under "View all plans") but is personalized on onboarding_goal_1: an animated mood image, a headline and one review that match the goal. The ratings, laurels and "No Payment Due Now" line are gone, and the price sits right above the button.

To build it
  • Page 3 switches on user.onboarding_goal_1 for stress, sleep and mood. Users who picked focus or gave no answer see the control's page 3.
  • Animations as Lottie files in the editor, or as CSS in a framework rebuild. Each one plays, holds on its final frame, then loops. With Reduce Motion on, each shows the final frame only.
C

Your program is ready

onboarding answers

By the paywall, people have answered about a dozen onboarding questions and watched Balance build their program, then they get a generic "7 days for free". Concept C cashes in that investment. Page 1 plays three of their own answers back. Page 2 shows the first week of their program, day by day at their reminder time, with the trial reminder placed on the day it lands. Page 3 sells the trial as "start your program". The offer is the control's.

To build it
  • The answers on page 1 are examples. Each line needs its onboarding answer sent to Superwall as a user attribute (how_often_feel_stress, source_of_stress, how_long_fall_asleep, keep_awake, has_meditated_before and the mood questions). Coverage of those attributes still needs checking; lines without an answer drop out.
  • The session names and minutes are placeholders. They should match what Balance's program builder gives each goal (the "Program ready" step).
  • The reminder time needs the onboarding reminder answer as an attribute. Without it, page 2 says "one session a day" with no time.
  • Page 2 drops the control's reminder choice: the reminder is fixed at 2 days before the trial ends (day 5) and shown in the week.
  • Focus-first and users without answers see the control. Facebook and Instagram users get the Meta hard paywall (253237) and never see this flow.
